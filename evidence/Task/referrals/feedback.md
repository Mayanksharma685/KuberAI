Mayank Sharma

### Feedback

> The specialised agent approach makes the product easy to understand. The Tax Agent is particularly useful because users can directly start with tax-related questions instead of figuring out which type of AI prompt to use.

### Key Takeaways

- Specialised agents make the product easier to navigate.
- The Tax Agent has a clear use case.
- The product should provide more example questions for first-time users.

### Potential Improvement

Add suggested questions to each agent's chat screen so users can immediately understand what they can ask.

### Feedback

> The interface is simple and the financial topics are clearly separated. The product would be stronger if it explained the limitations of AI-generated financial guidance more clearly and provided stronger trust and privacy messaging.

### Key Takeaways

- The simple interface is a strength.
- Clear financial guidance categories are useful.
- Trust and transparency are important for a financial AI product.

### Potential Improvement

Add clearer disclaimers, privacy messaging and guidance explaining when users should seek professional financial or tax advice.

# Combined Learnings

## What I Learned

The simulated referral exercise highlights two important areas: ease of navigation through specialised agents and the importance of trust when presenting financial information through AI.

## Changes I Would Prioritise

1. Add suggested questions to agent pages.
2. Strengthen trust and privacy messaging.
3. Make the limitations of AI-generated financial guidance clearer.
